How to Safely Humanize an Academic Thesis

1. The Winnowing Algorithm

Turnitin and iThenticate use the Winnowing algorithm with k=5 word sequences and Karp-Rabin rolling hashes. Any unbroken chain of 8+ words is flagged. Turnitout systematically alters 5-word windows using scholarly synonyms and clause shifts so that no 8-word sequence remains identical.

2. AI Detectors (Perplexity & Burstiness)

Detectors like GPTZero and Turnitin AI do NOT detect "truth"; they evaluate perplexity (how predictable words are) and burstiness (variation in sentence lengths). AI generates flat, uniform sentences. Turnitout introduces rhythmic variation and replaces high-probability AI clichés.

3. What Stays 100% Intact

Unlike ChatGPT or commercial paraphrasers, Turnitout uses zero generative AI. That means:

  • Facts, citations, and dates never change.
  • Equations, math symbols ($...$), and tables are protected.
  • IEEE [1] and APA (Smith, 2024) citations remain exact.
  • No hallucinations or fabricated references.

4. Best Practice for Thesis Defense

Always review the humanized output chapter-by-chapter. Use the Balanced (60%) mode for literature review and discussion sections, and keep your custom experimental parameters exact.
